Inside Joke

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

The short version

We keep hosts' accounts, purchases and game statistics. We don't keep what players say in a game: answers, secrets and the jokes made from them live in memory while the room is open and are deleted when it closes. Guests don't create accounts. We don't sell personal data and don't show ads.

Who we are

Inside Joke is operated by [Legal entity name], [registered address] ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: hello@insidejoke.app.

Hosts

When you sign in we store your email address, the name Google shares with us (if you use Google), your Google account identifier, when you signed in, your passes and their use, and a summary of each game you host (see "What we keep after a game"). We use this to sign you in, apply your pass, count free and monthly games, and support you.

To sign in with an email code we store a hashed copy of the code, the email it was sent to and the IP address that asked for it, to stop abuse. These records are deleted after 24 hours.

Guests

Guests only choose a display name and an emoji. While the room is open we hold the name, emoji, score and a random reconnect token in memory. Your browser keeps the token for up to 6 hours so you can rejoin after a dropped connection, remembers the last name and emoji you used, and keeps an unsent answer until you close the tab. Viewers in streamer mode get a random token and nothing else.

Game content

Answers, secrets and the host's lines exist only in the server's memory while the room is open. A room closes when the host ends it, after 30 minutes without activity, or after 4 hours at most; its content is then deleted. Audio of the host's voice is cached in memory for up to 10 minutes.

To run the game, the text players enter is sent to our AI providers: Anthropic, for writing rounds and jokes and for checking content, and [text-to-speech provider], which turns the host's lines into speech. They receive it only to return a result, under API terms that do not allow them to train their models on it.

Some topics are filtered automatically (health, appearance, sexual orientation, contact details and similar). Anyone a line is about can skip it, and the host can skip any line.

What we keep after a game

  • A game summary: tone, length, mode, number of players and rounds, number of secrets shared, peak number of viewers, when it started and ended, and why it ended.
  • AI usage records: which kind of request was made, its size in tokens or characters, cost, speed and whether it worked. Never the text.
  • Moderation records: at which step something was blocked or skipped, and the category. Never the text.
  • The host's rating of the game and an optional comment of up to 500 characters.

Payments

Payments are handled by Paddle.com, the merchant of record, which collects your payment details, billing information and tax data under its own privacy notice and sends your receipt. We receive the transaction identifier, the product, the amount and the currency, and we keep these purchase records for accounting and tax purposes, including after you delete your account.

Product analytics

We use PostHog to understand how the game is used, for example how many rooms are created, how many players join and how many games are finished. Events are linked to a random identifier: a host's account id, a room-scoped id for guests, or a random id stored in your browser on the landing page. They never contain names, answers or secrets.

Other service providers

Resend delivers sign-in emails. Google provides Google sign-in. Our servers are hosted by [hosting provider, region]. Each provider processes data only to provide its service to us.

Cookies and browser storage

We use a session cookie to keep hosts signed in (up to 30 days) and a security cookie that protects forms against cross-site requests. Local storage holds the room seats, preferences, drafts and analytics id described above. We use no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Legal bases

We process account, game and purchase data to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract); keep purchase records to meet tax and accounting obligations (legal obligation); and use security records and product analytics to protect and improve the service (legitimate interests).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, object to or restrict its use, and complain to your data protection authority. Hosts can delete their account at any time on the Account page: the email address and sign-in details are removed immediately, passes end, and open rooms close. For any other request, write to hello@insidejoke.app.

Children

Inside Joke is not intended for children under 13, and hosting, buying passes and the Spicy tone are for adults only. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell hosts by email or in the app.
