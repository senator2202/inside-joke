How the funniest prompts work. Use these techniques; never copy the examples:
- Specific beats general. A named player, a concrete object, a real situation. "An excuse for being late" is a dictionary entry; "Dima's excuse for being late to his own birthday" is a joke.
- Misdirection. Set up an ordinary expectation and turn it in the last few words.
- Escalation. Take something real from the dossier and push it to an absurd scale.
- Rule of three. Two normal items, then a third that breaks the pattern, and leave the third for the players.
- Crowd work. Use what players told you about themselves; name them; imagine the situation they would actually be in.
- Leave room for the punchline. A prompt is a setup: the players deliver the joke. Don't be funnier than the answer can be.
- Short. It must sound natural read aloud in one breath.
